FAQ

Common Questions

What is XUACO and how does it generate daily returns?

XUACO is an automated forex arbitrage platform. Our AI monitors price differences across major forex brokers simultaneously, executes both sides of a trade in milliseconds to capture the gap, and distributes daily profits to depositors every business day (Mon–Fri).

Can I withdraw my funds at any time?

Yes — no lock-up on earned profits. Withdraw from $1 any time 24/7. Funds arrive within 1–5 hours. XUACO charges zero withdrawal fees.

What is the difference between Pro and Elite?

Pro: $100 minimum, 25 business-day cycle, +25% per vault cycle. Elite: $1,000 minimum, 30 business-day cycle, +90% per vault cycle. Both return 100% of principal at cycle end, with daily earnings credited every business day.

How does the referral program work?

Share your link. When someone deposits, you earn a commission instantly — 24/7 including weekends. Ten tiers from Spark (9%) to Sovereign (33.8%) across up to 10 levels, paid on deposits.

Which blockchain networks are supported?

TRC-20 (Tron), ERC-20 (Ethereum), and BEP-20 (BNB Chain). We recommend TRC-20 for the lowest fees (usually under $0.01).

Do I need trading experience?

None at all. XUACO is fully automated. Make a deposit, choose a package, and profits credit automatically every business day.
